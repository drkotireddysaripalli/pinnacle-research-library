## Primary concurrent-validity family

For each prespecified comparison, report the paired denominator, Pearson correlation, 95% Fisher-z confidence interval, and the Bonferroni-adjusted threshold of 0.017. If the documented normality rule requires Spearman correlation, state the check and substitution. Keep Vineland-3 Adaptive Composite, CARS-2 Total, and the age-appropriate Bayley-4 or ABAS-3 comparison distinct.

## Test–retest reliability

Report the eligible repeat interval, achieved subset, ICC(3,1) with 95% confidence interval, SEM, Bland–Altman mean bias and limits of agreement. Document intervention or therapy changes during the 7 ± 2 day interval and apply the prespecified rules.

## Secondary and exploratory analyses

Report the preliminary MCID from the Global Impression of Change anchor, 0.5 SD and 1 SEM separately before any triangulation. Retain the label **preliminary**. Report classification concordance and ROC work as exploratory rather than diagnostic accuracy. State the multiple-comparison rule used for each family.

## Interpretation boundary

Separate observed results from protocol expectations, monograph findings and regulatory status. Discuss selection, spectrum, centre, rater and missing-pair limitations. A correlation does not by itself show interchangeability, diagnosis, treatment effectiveness or independent endorsement. No completed concurrent-validity result was public when this template was released.
