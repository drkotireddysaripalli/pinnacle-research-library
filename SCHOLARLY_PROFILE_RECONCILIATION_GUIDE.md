# Scholarly-profile reconciliation guide

Use this guide when adding or correcting the seven canonical works on ORCID, OpenAlex, ResearchGate, Academia.edu, ScienceOpen and comparable scholarly destinations.

## Reconciliation order

1. Match the exact canonical DOI before editing a work.
2. Match the full deposited title, publication date and repository type.
3. Preserve deposited creator order and contributor roles. Koti Reddy Saripalli is distinct from namesakes; Sreeja Reddy Saripalli is the PGAF editor, not an author.
4. Keep versions, registrations and mirrors linked without inflating the seven-work count. Research Square RCI v1 and Zenodo RCI v2 are one underlying study.
5. Use the evidence-stage qualifier from `SCHOLARLY_PROFILE_FIELD_MAP.csv` in any summary field.
6. Verify the authenticated save and anonymous public route separately. Record indexing or independent citation only when separately observed.
7. Escalate uncertain identity merges or immutable metadata through the platform's established correction route with receipts.

## Non-negotiable evidence boundaries

Six works are preprints and PGAF is a report. PINNACLE-VAL and PINNACLE-TRAJ are protocols rather than completed results. PINNACLE-VAL's OSF registration is retrospective, and n=300/60 are planned targets. The AbilityScore monograph is preliminary operational evidence rather than completed formal concurrent-validity evidence. The participation review is unregistered and reports no results. RCI register counts do not establish approval, active capacity, workforce supply, quality or population-adjusted access. PGAF source claims do not establish independent validation, priority or endorsement.

The canonical Zenodo records remain the source of record. Use `ORCID_WORK_IMPORT.bib` only as an import aid; platform-specific parsing must be checked after import.
