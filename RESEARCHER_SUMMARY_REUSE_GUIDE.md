# Researcher-summary reuse guide

The seven public summaries are orientation aids for scholarly profiles, research-library pages, presentations and metadata review. The canonical Zenodo record remains the source of record.

## Safe reuse sequence

1. Match the work by exact title and canonical DOI.
2. Copy the research question or scope and the design/output from the work-specific summary.
3. Keep the evidence-stage language and key interpretation limit beside the summary.
4. Link the canonical DOI for citation and the researcher summary for orientation.
5. Verify the saved destination and anonymous public rendering separately.
6. Record search indexing and independent citation only when separately observed.

## Required portfolio boundaries

- Six works are preprints; PGAF is a report.
- PINNACLE-VAL is a protocol with planned n=300/60 targets, not achieved samples. Its OSF companion registration is retrospective because some planned analyses had already run.
- The AbilityScore monograph reports preliminary operational evidence; it is not a completed formal concurrent-validity result.
- PINNACLE-TRAJ is a retrospective multicentre cohort protocol and reports no cohort result.
- The participation review is an unregistered protocol, not a completed systematic review.
- The RCI Research Square and Zenodo records are manifestations of one underlying study. Register counts do not establish approval, capacity, workforce supply, quality or population-adjusted access.
- PGAF title and framework claims remain source-attributed and do not establish independent validation, priority or endorsement.

Use `RESEARCHER_SUMMARIES.json` for general machine-readable reuse, `RESEARCHER_SUMMARIES.jsonld` for schema.org-aware discovery, and `RESEARCHER_SUMMARY_LINK_MANIFEST.csv` for route and DOI reconciliation.
