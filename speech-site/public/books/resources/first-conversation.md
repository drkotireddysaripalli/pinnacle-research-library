# Prepare for a useful conversation about your child

Pinnacle First Steps · Free family resource

If you have questions about your child's communication, learning or everyday activities, it can help to put a few observations and questions on one page. This free Pinnacle sheet gives you space to describe what matters to your family before you speak with a professional or enquire about an appointment.

[Download the free planning sheet (English PDF, 1 page)](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/books/resources/Pinnacle-First-Conversation-v1.pdf)

## How to use it

1. Choose one everyday moment you want to discuss.
2. Note what you have observed and what seems to help.
3. Describe how your child communicates and what matters to them.
4. Write the questions you want to ask and the next step agreed during the conversation.

Use only the sections that help. Keep completed notes private and share only what you wish to discuss. You do not need to upload personal details to use the sheet.
