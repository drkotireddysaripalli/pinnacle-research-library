# Pinnacle SEVA — developmental therapy access and family support

Canonical: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/seva
Reviewed: 4 October 2026
Publisher: Pinnacle Blooms Network, operated by Bharath Healthcare Laboratories Private Limited.

SEVA means Social Equity in Valuable Access. It is Pinnacle's programme for helping families explore developmental therapy support when cost is a barrier. Published programme materials describe a subsidised-access model centred on dignity.

## Current support

Call 9100 181 181 (tel:+919100181181) to ask what support is available now. Confirm eligibility, documents, participating locations, any fees and appointment availability for your family before travelling. Reading this page or making an enquiry does not confirm a subsidy or a place. Earlier published criteria are not an automatic entitlement today.

## Your conversation

1. Describe what matters in your child's everyday life and what makes access difficult.
2. Clarify current support, eligibility, documents, costs and the appropriate centre.
3. Agree the next professional conversation and how the plan and support terms will be reviewed.

Ask what you would pay, for which services, and for how long. You do not need to upload personal or financial documents to read this page. Optional free preparation sheet: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/books/resources/first-conversation

## The child's life remains the purpose

The child's self-sufficient, mainstream-included life shapes abilities, goals, suitable people and methods, everyday practice and review. SEVA concerns access. Professional assessment and the child-specific plan determine suitable clinical support; it is not a fixed therapy package. Explore https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacleai and https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/centers.
