## Occupational therapy

Claim: Occupational therapy can support participation in meaningful everyday activities by considering the child, activity and environment.
Source: https://wfot.org/about/about-occupational-therapy
Scope: General professional description. Individual assessment determines suitable care; WFOT does not endorse Pinnacle.

Claim: Children's everyday occupations may include self-care, education, play and social participation.
Source: https://www.aota.org/practice/domain-and-process/occupations-everyday-activities
Scope: General framework, not a Pinnacle outcome or AOTA endorsement.
