A turn in a game can connect with life beyond it.
“My child loves this game. What might help them take part with another child?”
Illustrative process:
1. Begin with an interest.
Bring a game your child enjoys or describe what makes it engaging. Notice the communication, movement and help already involved.
2. Choose participation as the goal.
Discuss a suitable way to share a turn or express a choice. Relevant support follows that individual priority.
3. Connect practice across settings.
Ask how a family member can guide a familiar activity, and whether teacher observations would be useful with your consent.
4. Review, then decide.
Compare what happened with different people and support. Keep, adjust or reconsider the plan with the professional rather than assuming the activity alone proves an outcome.
