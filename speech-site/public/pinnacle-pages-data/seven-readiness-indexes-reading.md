# Seven Readiness Indexes | A Fuller View of Your Child’s Next Step
Canonical: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/seven-readiness-indexes

> The BIS record names School, Speech, Motor, Study IQ, Behavior, Self Sufficiency and Mainstream Inclusion Readiness Indexes. These are separate software views that support discussion and planning; they do not certify school entry, intelligence, social worth or future independence.

## Why this matters
Readiness is useful when it points to a meaningful next opportunity in the child’s life and the support needed to try it.

## One illustrative everyday moment
A classroom goal has more than one doorway.
Illustrative example only. A child enjoys group play but needs help asking a peer to join.
1. **Notice strengths:** The child may already follow a routine and enjoy peers.
2. **Look at relevant views:** Communication and school participation might matter here; the other views remain context rather than automatic targets.
3. **Choose suitable support:** A professional and family agree on an accessible invitation method and what the school can do.
4. **Review in real life:** The team asks whether the child could join and enjoy play, not whether an index alone improved.

## Boundary
The names are those printed in the BIS schedule. They should not be read as a formal school admission decision or a promise of mainstream placement. Report versions have used different display scales; use the current report and professional explanation.
