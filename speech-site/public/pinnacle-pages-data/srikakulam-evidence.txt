An interest at home can shape a useful learning goal.
“My child enjoys drawing at home. What could help them join a short activity with another person?”
Illustrative process:
1. Begin with your child’s interest.
Describe what your child enjoys, how they choose materials and what helps them stay involved. Notice communication, movement, comfort and support.
2. Define useful participation.
Discuss a suitable next step, such as sharing a choice or joining part of an activity. Relevant support follows that everyday purpose.
3. Connect family and learning settings.
Ask for manageable guidance. With your consent, relevant teacher observations can add another perspective.
4. Review the experience.
Discuss participation, comfort and the support involved before deciding whether to continue or adjust the plan.
