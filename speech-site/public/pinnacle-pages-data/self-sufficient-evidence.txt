Growing self-sufficiency means taking a greater part in everyday life with the support that fits your child. It includes making choices, communicating needs, managing routines and knowing when to ask for help. A useful plan starts with the life you want to make possible, then chooses the relevant goals, people, methods and review.

What does self-sufficiency mean for a child?
It means growing participation in everyday choices and routines with appropriate support. Communication, self-care, learning and asking for help can all contribute. The meaning and goals are individual to the child.

Does my child have to do everything without help?
No. Useful support, adaptations and asking for help can be part of independence. The family and professional discuss which activities matter, what the child can do and what support helps.

How does Pinnacle start?
Start with one everyday moment you want to make more possible. Discuss present abilities, a suitable assessment, meaningful priorities, the people and support involved, family practice and how progress would be reviewed.

Does every child need all the therapies?
No. Assessment and the child-specific priorities guide the relevant professional contributions. Speech, occupational, behavioural or educational support may play different roles; a fixed bundle is not assumed.

Do PinnacleAI or the licences guarantee independence?
No. PinnacleAI GPT-OS is licensed non-diagnostic developmental-support software. MD-5 and BIS describe their printed scopes. They do not guarantee a child’s independence, school admission or outcome.
