Autism Therapy and Developmental Support for Children — claims and sources
Checked: 29 September 2026
Canonical: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/autism-therapy

1. Autism diversity and individual support
Source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/autism-spectrum-disorders
Use: autism is diverse; abilities and support needs vary and can change.
Limit: no WHO endorsement of Pinnacle and no determination of an individual plan.

2. Individual and multidisciplinary planning
Sources: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg170/chapter/Recommendations and https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg128/chapter/Recommendations
Use: consider strengths, communication, health, daily living, sensory conditions, learning, environment and family context.
Limit: no compulsory therapy bundle and no proof of an individual outcome.

3. Diagnosis boundary
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/autism/diagnosis/index.html
Use: diagnosis relies on developmental history and qualified professional observation.
Limit: a call, form or PinnacleAI record does not diagnose autism.

4. Pinnacle life-first direction
Source: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/verify/evidence/pinnacle-paradigm-shift.html
Use: connect abilities, goals, relevant people, everyday practice and review with the child’s wider life.
Limit: no guarantee of speech, independence, school admission, mainstream inclusion or a particular result.

5. PinnacleAI regulatory scope
Sources: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/verify/evidence/records/md5.html, https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/verify/evidence/records/bis.html and https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/verify/evidence/records/fsc.html
Use: non-diagnostic developmental-support Class B SaMD for children aged 0–12, with documented measurement, readiness, forecasting and plan-support functions.
Limit: no autism diagnosis, professional credential, centre registration, foreign approval or outcome guarantee is inferred.

6. Research and centres
Sources: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/verify/evidence/research-library.html and https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/verify/evidence/centre-entity-reference.html
Use: inspect study statuses and dated centre identities, addresses and maps.
Limit: confirm the service, professional, appointment and current fees before travel.
