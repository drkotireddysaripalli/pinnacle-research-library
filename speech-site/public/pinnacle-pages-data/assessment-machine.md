> Understand your child today. Choose a clearer next step for life.

Communication. Play. Daily routines. Learning. Start with what your child can do and what you want to make more possible.

## What assessment clarifies
A child development assessment brings family priorities, professional observation and suitable measures together to understand present abilities and support needs. The aim is an explained starting picture and a useful next-step discussion—not a score on its own.

## Life-first direction
Pinnacle’s life-first direction begins with the child’s growing self-sufficiency and mainstream participation.

## An illustrative everyday example
Assessment can connect the family’s question with a relevant ability and an explained next step. This example shows that reasoning; it is not a patient story or a prescription.
Notice a strength. Understand the support. Choose a useful next opportunity.
### 1. Begin with the family’s moment
“My child enjoys playing near other children. How can we help them invite someone to join?”
### 2. Notice one usable ability
In this illustration, the child offers a favourite toy to a familiar adult during comfortable play at home, after the adult holds out a hand. There is a starting ability to build on, and the person, setting and cue matter.
### 3. Interpret the ability in context
A professional considers this supported way of beginning shared play alongside the child’s age, interests, communication, comfort and family observations. They discuss what it could mean for inviting a play partner and what still needs to be understood.
### 4. Choose the next opportunity together
For this example, the family chooses a brief opportunity with a familiar, willing play partner and the same favourite toy, if appropriate for the child. With the professional, they agree an accessible gesture, picture or word to explore, while respecting the child’s choice to join or pause.
### 5. Review what became useful
Review asks whether the child used an invitation, with whom and where, how comfortable the moment was, and what help remained. If it worked only with the original adult and cue, that context stays visible while the family and professional decide whether to continue or adapt the next opportunity.

## Seven connected stages
1. **[Understand abilities](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/abilityscore):** Start with strengths, everyday observations and suitable assessment. Read AbilityScore with the child’s age, context and skills.
2. **[Plan for readiness](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/seven-readiness-indexes):** Discuss what you want to make possible and agree useful priorities. Readiness views support professional judgement.
3. **[Choose relevant support](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/autism-therapy):** Bring in the therapies, educational support or health review that fit this child. Every child does not need every service.
4. **[Practise in everyday life](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/everyday-therapy):** Use manageable, guided practice in meaningful routines. Family participation connects the work with daily life.
5. **[Track and correct](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/fusion-module):** Bring relevant observations back into review. Ask what became useful, what support remains and what needs to change.
6. **[Reassess and repeat](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/reassess-review-repeat):** Compare abilities and context over time. Continue, adapt or complete support according to the child’s needs.
7. **[Grow independence and participation](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacleai):** Keep self-sufficiency, learning, relationships and mainstream participation in view. Progress is individual.

## Questions before booking
### What is a child development assessment?
It is a structured way to understand a child’s present abilities and support needs using family priorities, professional observation and suitable measures. The assessment should lead to an explained starting picture and a useful next-step discussion.

### Do I need to choose a therapy before I call?
No. Describe one everyday moment and what you want to make more possible. The team can help check which professional, assessment or other route is suitable. A child does not automatically need every therapy.

### Does this assessment diagnose autism or ADHD?
An enquiry, AbilityScore or PinnacleAI software record does not diagnose autism or ADHD. If a diagnostic question needs answering, ask about the appropriately qualified professional or diagnostic pathway. Developmental-support assessment and medical diagnosis have different purposes.

### What does AbilityScore show?
AbilityScore uses a 0–1000 composite developmental ability scale. Professionals read it with age, assessed skills and everyday observations. Its public methodology is a preprint and the external-validation record is a protocol; a score does not decide care by itself.

### Which ages and assessment formats are available?
The receiving team confirms the suitable service, professional, age range and format before your appointment. The licensed PinnacleAI software scope is ages 0–12; that does not mean every assessment format is available for every child or at every centre.

### Is the assessment free?
Pinnacle’s confirmed FREE offer applies to the speech and language assessment campaign. General developmental assessments and other services may have different fees. Ask the receiving team to confirm the exact service, current fee and appointment before your visit.

### What should I bring and what will I receive?
Bring your questions and existing reports you want the professional to consider. Before booking, ask what the assessment involves, who conducts it, what explanation or written output is included and when the next review is discussed. Save detailed child information for the private professional conversation.

### How does assessment connect to a more independent life?
The everyday life your child is growing toward gives assessment a purpose. Present abilities help the family and professional choose relevant goals, support and manageable practice. Review asks whether those abilities become useful at home, in learning and with other people. No particular result or timetable is guaranteed.
