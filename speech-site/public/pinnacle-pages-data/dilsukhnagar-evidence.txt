From choosing a toy to joining everyday play.
“My child reaches for a favourite toy. How can we help them make a choice and share that choice with us?”
Illustrative process:
1. Start with what your child already does.
Bring an example of how your child chooses, asks or takes part. A suitable professional can observe the communication and support involved.
2. Choose a useful life goal.
Discuss a way to express a choice during a familiar game. The goal comes first; relevant communication, play or other support follows.
3. Try it in a familiar setting.
Agree a manageable opportunity at home, the help to offer and what to notice. The aim is useful participation outside the centre.
4. Review what changed.
Bring back what happened with different people or activities. The professional and family use those observations to adjust the next step.
