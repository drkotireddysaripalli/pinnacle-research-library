A family meal can give the work a direction.
“My child has a favourite snack. How can choosing it and joining us become easier?”
Illustrative process:
1. Recognise a useful starting point.
Describe how your child chooses, reaches, communicates or stays with the family during a familiar meal. Include what already helps.
2. Choose a meaningful next step.
Discuss one way your child could participate more. Relevant communication, daily-routine or sensory support follows the goal; no compulsory therapy bundle is assumed.
3. Keep practice familiar.
Ask for a suitable, manageable idea for your own family routine. Comfort, choice and the child’s participation guide what you try.
4. Bring real observations into review.
Discuss what happened with familiar food, people and support. The family and professional decide whether to keep, adapt or reconsider the next step.

Use manageable, guided practice in meaningful routines. Family participation connects the work with daily life.

5. Track and correct
Bring relevant observations back into review. Ask what became useful, what support remains and what needs to change.
https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/fusion-module
6. Reassess and repeat
Compare abilities and context over time. Continue, adapt or complete support according to the child’s needs.

Keep self-sufficiency, learning, relationships and mainstream participation in view. Progress is individual.
