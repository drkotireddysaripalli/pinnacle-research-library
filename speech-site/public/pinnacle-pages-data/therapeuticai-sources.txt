Parent moment: “My child wants to play together. Why is this activity in the plan?”
Decision this page explains: Which activity fits the child’s goal and comfort, who chose it, and what would count as useful change in ordinary life?
Family discussion: A clear reason for a guided activity and a way to discuss the child’s response with the professional.

Direct answer: The BIS schedule describes Everyday Therapy delivered through TherapeuticAI and an adaptive therapy-plan support function. Professional assessment and family priorities determine what is suitable. The software supports selection, delivery and tracking; it does not independently prescribe treatment.
Life purpose: Speech, occupational, behavioural and educational contributions may each be useful when they serve a particular child’s daily participation; no fixed combination is assumed.
Boundary: A BIS licence and module name do not certify an individual therapist, establish treatment effectiveness or mean every therapy is appropriate for every child.

Illustrative example: From requesting help to joining play.
Illustrative example only. A child wants help opening a play box so a shared game can begin.
1. Name the life moment: The child’s wish to join play gives the activity its purpose.
2. Select with judgement: A relevant professional chooses an accessible communication or motor support and checks comfort and preference.
3. Practise with meaning: The technique is used in the session and an agreed natural moment, rather than repeated for a score alone.
4. Review response: Family and therapist discuss what the child used, what support remained and whether to adapt.
