# Prognose | A Revisable Forecast for Child-Specific Planning
Canonical: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/prognose

> Make a plan for tomorrow. Let today’s child revise it.

Your child wants to join a playground game. A useful forecast helps the team choose a possible next step and decide what to watch for. Prognose supports that planning conversation; later observations can change the estimate and the support.

## The family question
“What could we try next, and how will we know if it fits?”

## What this page helps decide
Which starting information supports this tentative goal, what will be observed and when should the team reconsider?

## What the module does
Prognose is the named forecasting function in the PinnacleAI/BIS module map. It can support goal selection and checkpoints based on assessed information. It does not foretell a child’s future, prescribe a fixed therapy dose or guarantee a score by a date.

## One illustrative everyday moment
Preparing for a playground invitation.
Illustrative example only. A child wants to join a familiar game with another child.
1. **Describe now:** The team understands the child’s communication, motor access, preferences and environment.
2. **Set a testable next step:** A short goal might be a comfortable way to initiate or accept one invitation.
3. **Choose checkpoints:** Family and professionals agree what they will observe in relevant real settings.
4. **Revise the estimate:** If the child responds differently than forecast, the team changes support or timing instead of blaming the child.
