# Prognose | PinnacleAI Progress Forecasting & Planning
Canonical: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/prognose

What could help your child join the next game, routine or learning moment? Prognose brings a possible next step into the planning conversation—and keeps it open to what your child actually does.

## What Prognose does
Prognose is PinnacleAI’s named progress-forecasting module. It supports a revisable planning estimate using relevant assessed information and the child’s context. A professional and family consider the next goal, the support to try and what later observations should change.

## Purpose from the beginning
Your child’s self-sufficient, mainstream-included life gives the journey its direction. We connect that purpose with an understandable goal, suitable support and a point to review together.

## One illustrative playground decision
Illustrative planning example: a five-year-old enjoys rolling a ball with a familiar adult. The family’s priority is a comfortable way to take part with another child. This is a fictional explanation, not a patient result or software prediction.

## Build on something the child enjoys.
The child chooses the ball, rolls it with a familiar adult and can indicate “more” or “stop”. The team considers communication, movement, comfort and the playground setting.

## Could one invitation be a useful next goal?
With an agreed picture cue and adult support, the team considers whether the child might accept an invitation from a familiar peer. This is a possibility to explore, not a predicted result.

## The response changes the discussion.
The child rolls the ball twice with the adult in a quiet area, then steps back and indicates “stop” when several children gather nearby. That records a choice and a change of setting; it does not establish why the response changed.

## The professional decision changes
The professional and family revise the next checkpoint: offer one familiar peer a place in the quieter area, retain the agreed cue and easy “stop” choice, and observe whether the child wants to accept or initiate one turn. Participation in the larger group is not the immediate checkpoint.

## The next observation
At the agreed review, describe whether an invitation was offered, accepted or declined; what help was used; the setting; and the child’s comfort. The response to this revised support has not yet happened in the example.

## If the child chooses to take a turn
Record the cue and adult help still needed. Review whether to repeat the same opportunity, gradually change one condition or choose another meaningful goal. One supported turn is not generalised independent group play.

## If the child declines or needs a pause
Respect that response. Review the activity, support, setting or timing with the family; an alternative way to participate may be more useful. The plan changes around the child.

## What is Prognose in PinnacleAI?
Prognose is PinnacleAI’s named progress-forecasting module. It supports a revisable estimate for goal selection and checkpoints using relevant assessed information and context, with professional interpretation and family involvement.

## What should my family understand about a forecast?
Ask what information supports the proposed goal, which assumptions matter, what support will be tried, what will be observed and when the professional will reconsider the plan. The next step should be understandable to your family.

## Can Prognose predict when my child will be independent?
It cannot promise an individual endpoint or a date for independence, school entry or mainstream inclusion. Those remain meaningful directions for care; a forecast is a planning aid that must be reviewed against the child’s actual response.

## What happens if my child responds differently than expected?
The professional reviews the observation, setting and support with the family. The next goal, activity, support or timing may change. In the illustrative playground example, the revised checkpoint is a chosen turn with one familiar peer in a quieter area, rather than larger-group play.

## Does a child have to continue an activity to meet the forecast?
No. The child’s comfort, preferences and accessible ways to pause or decline matter. A forecast is not a quota or a reason to force participation; a professional can adapt or pause the plan.

## How does Prognose connect with the other PinnacleAI modules?
Ability and readiness information and PDK context inform the planning discussion. Suitable activities and everyday practice follow professional selection. Fusion feedback and later reassessment can change the estimate and next step.

## Is Prognose a diagnosis or a proven prediction for every child?
No. PinnacleAI’s licensed use is non-diagnostic developmental support. The BIS schedule names Prognose, while the linked AbilityScore external-validation record is a protocol, not completed external predictive validation for Prognose.
