What Fusion does
Fusion is PinnacleAI’s tracking and correction module. It connects relevant observations from therapy, home and, where appropriate and consented, school with professional review. The useful output is a decision about what to keep, adapt, pause or investigate, followed by a clear next observation.

Purpose from the beginning
Your child’s self-sufficient, mainstream-included life is the purpose from the beginning. Fusion helps bring the child’s everyday experience back into the next professional decision, with your family helping shape the journey.

One request across three settings
Illustrative example: a five-year-old enjoys taking turns with a ball. The family wants their child to be able to ask for a turn and take part comfortably. This fictional example explains a review; it is not a patient report or a personalised therapy plan.

01 · Therapy · A request with a familiar cue.
Setting: Quiet room; one familiar professional.
Adult support: The adult points to the “my turn” picture before offering the ball.
Child response: The child touches the picture and takes a turn.
Interpretation: A supported request in this setting—not yet an independent request.

02 · Home · The same cue still matters.
Setting: Ball play with a parent in a familiar room.
Adult support: The parent keeps the picture within reach and points to it.
Child response: The child uses the picture after the reminder, then chooses another turn.
Interpretation: The request appears with another adult, with the cue still present.

03 · School, if shared · A different response in a busy space.
Setting: Several children playing outdoors; teacher observation shared with consent.
Adult support: The same picture is available, with an adult invitation.
Child response: The child steps away and does not take the offered turn.
Interpretation: The observation does not establish why. Noise, timing, comfort and support need review.

Compare context
The picture-supported request appears in two quieter, familiar situations. It has not yet been observed without an adult cue, or in the busy playground. These are different questions; averaging them into one “success” score would lose information the next decision needs.

The professional decision
In this example, the professional and family keep the picture cue, choose a quieter place with one familiar peer, and make a way to pause or decline available. The immediate checkpoint becomes a chosen turn with that support—not joining the larger group.

Next checkpoint
At the next agreed opportunity, note the setting, who was there, the cue offered, how the child responded and whether the child chose to continue or pause. The response to the revised support has not yet been observed.

If the supported turn is comfortable
Keep the useful support and review another relevant opportunity. A supported turn does not by itself establish independent requesting or transfer to a larger group.

If the child steps away again
Respect the pause, reduce the demand and bring the observation back. The professional can reconsider communication, comfort, the environment or the goal, and decide whether other assessment or referral is needed.

Roles and access
School or other outside input requires appropriate consent and authorised access. Ask the professional how to share observations in the current care programme; private records stay in the agreed care channel.

What is the Fusion Module in PinnacleAI?
Fusion is PinnacleAI’s tracking and correction module. It brings relevant observations from therapy, home and, where appropriate and consented, school into professional review so the team can decide what to keep, adapt, pause or investigate next.

Why can a skill work in therapy but look different at home or school?
The people, environment, timing, activity and support may differ. Fusion’s review keeps those details with the child’s response so the professional can investigate the difference instead of treating all observations as equivalent.

What should a parent bring into a progress review?
Bring one specific everyday example: what was happening, where, who was present, what support or cue was offered and how your child responded. Include the child’s choices and comfort. Share it through the agreed care channel, not in public comments.

Does Fusion automatically change my child’s therapy?
No. Relevant observations inform professional interpretation. The professional discusses appropriate changes with the family and explains what to observe next; a rating alone is not an autonomous treatment decision.

Does every child need input from school or several different raters?
No. Relevant contributors depend on the child’s circumstances. School input requires appropriate consent and authorised access; the described rater roles do not mean that every role contributes to every child’s record.

What if my child does not want to continue an activity?
The child’s choice, comfort and accessible way to pause or decline belong in the review. The professional can adapt or pause the support and reconsider the goal. Completing an activity is not the only useful information.

How does Fusion connect with reassessment and the other therapies?
Fusion connects everyday observations with the next support decision. Reassessment compares the broader ability and readiness picture over time. Speech, occupational, behavioural and educational contributions are included according to the child’s goals and professional judgement, not as a compulsory bundle.

Do the licences guarantee progress for my child?
The BIS schedule names Fusion within the software quality-system scope. MD-5 defines PinnacleAI’s non-diagnostic developmental-support intended use. These records do not guarantee an individual outcome; your child’s actual response remains central to review.
