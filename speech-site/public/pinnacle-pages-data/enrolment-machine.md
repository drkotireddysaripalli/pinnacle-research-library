# Enrol at Pinnacle | A Life-First Path for Your Child

Canonical: https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/enroll-autism-speech-aba-therapies-india

This page begins a conversation with Pinnacle Blooms Network. A family may provide a name and mobile number, then optionally share the service, centre or everyday priority they want to discuss. The form does not diagnose a child or commit the family to a service.

Pinnacle begins with the life the family wants the child to grow toward: greater independence and participation in family, learning, school and community. Assessment, suitable therapies, family-guided everyday practice, progress review and correction are connected to that direction.

## What happens next

1. The Pinnacle team receives the enquiry when the system explicitly confirms acceptance.
2. The team calls to understand the family’s priorities.
3. The team confirms an appropriate centre, professional, appointment and current fees.
4. The family decides the next step after the conversation.
