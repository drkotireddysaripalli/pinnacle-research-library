# PinnacleAI® product and module pages

> Reading aid for the public, source-linked PinnacleAI pages. It does not guarantee AI ingestion, citation or endorsement.

- [PinnacleAI®](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacleai): Explore the PinnacleAI Paradigm Shift: measurement, integrated therapies, family practice and review towards a self-sufficient, mainstream-included life. 9100 181 181. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/pinnacleai-sources.json).
- [AbilityScore®](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/abilityscore): AbilityScore helps a professional discuss what a child can do now and which everyday goal may come next. See a toy-choice example, 0–1000 limits and sources. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/abilityscore-sources.json).
- [7 Readiness Indexes](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/seven-readiness-indexes): Explore seven PinnacleAI Readiness views through a child’s peer-play goal. See what each view can help discuss, its limits and the BIS source. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/seven-readiness-indexes-sources.json).
- [Personal Development Kernel](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/personal-development-kernel): See how PinnacleAI’s Personal Development Kernel keeps a child’s home and school context available for authorised professional review. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/personal-development-kernel-sources.json).
- [Prognose](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/prognose): Prognose supports a revisable next goal and checkpoint. Follow a playground example and see how observation can change the plan. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/prognose-sources.json).
- [TherapeuticAI®](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/therapeuticai): TherapeuticAI connects a child’s life goal to professionally chosen activity, guided use and response review. Follow one play-box example. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/therapeuticai-sources.json).
- [Everyday Therapy™](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/everyday-therapy): Everyday Therapy brings one professionally guided step into an ordinary family routine. See a mealtime example and how observations return to review. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/everyday-therapy-sources.json).
- [Fusion](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/fusion-module): Fusion brings relevant therapy, home and consented school observations into human review, so the child’s next support can change with evidence. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/fusion-module-sources.json).
- [Reassess · Review · Repeat](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/reassess-review-repeat): Reassess, Review and Repeat compares a child’s everyday skill over time and helps the family and team decide what to keep or change. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/reassess-review-repeat-sources.json).

PinnacleAI GPT-OS v1.0.0 is Class B non-diagnostic developmental-support software under the Indian MD-5 scope for ages 0–12. Operator: Bharath Healthcare Laboratories Private Limited; brand: Pinnacle Blooms Network. The seven-stage family journey is an explanation of programme direction, not a guaranteed outcome.
