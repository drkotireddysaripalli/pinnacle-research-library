- [AbilityScore®](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/abilityscore): See how AbilityScore’s 0–1000 developmental measure helps explain your child’s abilities and next step. Explore the method, family role and evidence. 9100 181 181. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/abilityscore-sources.json).
- [7 Readiness Indexes](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/seven-readiness-indexes): Understand PinnacleAI’s seven readiness indexes for school, communication, movement, learning and everyday life. Explore the evidence and call 9100 181 181. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/seven-readiness-indexes-sources.json).
- [Personal Development Kernel](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/personal-development-kernel): See how PinnacleAI’s Personal Development Kernel keeps a child’s home and school context available for authorised professional review. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/personal-development-kernel-sources.json).
- [Prognose](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/prognose): Prognose supports a revisable next goal and checkpoint. Follow a playground example and see how observation can change the plan. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/prognose-sources.json).
- [TherapeuticAI®](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/therapeuticai): TherapeuticAI connects a child’s life goal to professionally chosen activity, guided use and response review. Follow one play-box example. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/therapeuticai-sources.json).
- [Everyday Therapy™](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/everyday-therapy): Guided practice, family observations and professional review: follow one table-setting goal from the child’s plan into a meaningful everyday moment. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/everyday-therapy-sources.json).
- [Fusion](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/fusion-module): Fusion brings relevant therapy, home and consented school observations into human review, so the child’s next support can change with evidence. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/fusion-module-sources.json).
- [Reassess · Review · Repeat](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/reassess-review-repeat): Reassess, Review and Repeat compares a child’s everyday skill over time and helps the family and team decide what to keep or change. [Sources](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacle-pages-data/reassess-review-repeat-sources.json).
