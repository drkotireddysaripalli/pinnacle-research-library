## Enquiry, assessment and consent

Calling or sending an enquiry asks our team to contact you; it does not by itself enrol a child or confirm a slot. Before proceeding, discuss what the visit includes, who will meet your child, suitable support and the consent required. We do not assume every child needs every therapy.

The child’s growing self-sufficiency and participation give the work its direction. Goals and progress remain individual. Software, facility registration and professional qualifications have different scopes.

- The price, currency, taxes or additional charges, payment instructions and what is included.

- Appointment, cancellation, rescheduling, unused-session and refund conditions.

- How the family participates, receives guidance and discusses progress or a concern.

Any booking-specific condition must be supplied before agreement. A public promotion applies only on its stated scope and terms; free telephone guidance does not mean every assessment or therapy is free.

Provide information you are authorised to share, tell the team about relevant needs and ask when a plan or charge is unclear. Our personnel must respect the child’s dignity, preference, communication, comfort and privacy, explain relevant choices and keep records truthful. Suitable support and reviews are professional decisions with family participation; an outcome is not guaranteed.
