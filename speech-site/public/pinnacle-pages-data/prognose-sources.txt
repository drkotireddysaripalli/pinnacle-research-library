Prognose | A Revisable Forecast for Child-Specific Planning
https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/prognose

Parent moment: “What could we try next, and how will we know if it fits?”
Decision this page explains: Which starting information supports this tentative goal, what will be observed and when should the team reconsider?
Family discussion: A revisable plan with an understood checkpoint, not a predicted date for independence.

Direct answer: Prognose is the named forecasting function in the PinnacleAI/BIS module map. It can support goal selection and checkpoints based on assessed information. It does not foretell a child’s future, prescribe a fixed therapy dose or guarantee a score by a date.
Life purpose: The forecast matters only if it helps the team choose a meaningful next step and notice early when the plan needs to change.
Boundary: Current public Verify records do not establish a completed external predictive-validation result. A forecast is decision support, not an independently proven prediction for an individual child.

Illustrative example: Preparing for a playground invitation.
Illustrative example only. A child wants to join a familiar game with another child.
1. Describe now: The team understands the child’s communication, motor access, preferences and environment.
2. Set a testable next step: A short goal might be a comfortable way to initiate or accept one invitation.
3. Choose checkpoints: Family and professionals agree what they will observe in relevant real settings.
4. Revise the estimate: If the child responds differently than forecast, the team changes support or timing instead of blaming the child.
