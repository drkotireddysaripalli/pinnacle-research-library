## Our conduct standard

Pinnacle Blooms Network is operated by Bharath Healthcare Laboratories Private Limited (BHCL). In these policies, “we”, “us” and “our” mean BHCL in its operation of Pinnacle Blooms Network.

Our people must keep the child’s growing independence and participation at the centre of their work. These public conduct standards explain our expectations; employment-specific terms and procedures belong in the applicable employment documents. This page does not establish an automated tribunal, disciplinary score or certification of every practice.

## Dignity, safety and professional scope

- Respect the child’s comfort, preference and communication, and the family’s dignity. Do not use shame, threats, coercive promises or fabricated outcome claims.

- Work within the relevant role, competence and authorised service. Do not misrepresent qualifications or use a software licence as a professional credential.

- Explain relevant choices, obtain appropriate permissions and report safety or safeguarding concerns through the responsible channels.

## Records, confidentiality and accountability

- Keep assessments, attendance, observations and billing records truthful. Do not backdate, fabricate a result or conceal a material error.

- Access or share personal information only for an authorised purpose. Use approved channels; do not put child records into public chat, personal marketing or social media.

- Disclose relevant conflicts and do not seek improper gifts, commissions or review incentives.

- Take family concerns seriously, cooperate with reviews and do not retaliate for a good-faith concern.

## Raise a conduct concern

Email [care@pinnacleblooms.org](mailto:care@pinnacleblooms.org) with the subject “Staff conduct or care concern”, a way to contact you and the information needed to understand your request. You can call [9100 181 181](tel:+919100181181) for guidance. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full card details or unnecessary child records.

Give the relevant location, role or service and a brief account. A concern is reviewed by responsible management; a privacy matter can be directed to Mr. Gokul Krishna Rao as Privacy Grievance Officer. The applicable process must protect confidentiality and fairness.
