## Life-first direction
The child’s self-sufficient, mainstream-included life shapes the abilities to understand, goals, people, methods, everyday practice and review. Progress remains individual.

## Illustrative everyday example
Getting ready can become a step toward independence.
“My child wants to go out with us. How can getting dressed become something they take part in?”
- **See the starting ability.** — Describe how your child chooses clothes, reaches, moves or asks for help. Comfort and the support already working matter.
- **Choose one useful part of the routine.** — Agree a meaningful goal, such as joining one step of dressing. Movement, communication, sensory comfort and sequencing can contribute together.
- **Make the plan fit your morning.** — Ask for a manageable way to practise in your real routine. A family should understand the purpose and the help to offer.
- **Track participation and support.** — Bring back what your child did, what became easier and which help still mattered. Review before increasing the challenge.

## Seven connected stages
1. **[Understand abilities](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/abilityscore)** — Start with strengths, everyday observations and suitable assessment. Read AbilityScore with the child’s age, context and skills.
2. **[Plan for readiness](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/seven-readiness-indexes)** — Discuss what you want to make possible and agree useful priorities. Readiness views support professional judgement.
3. **[Choose relevant support](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/autism-therapy)** — Bring in the therapies, educational support or health review that fit this child. Every child does not need every service.
4. **[Practise in everyday life](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/everyday-therapy)** — Use manageable, guided practice in meaningful routines. Family participation connects the work with daily life.
5. **[Track and correct](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/fusion-module)** — Bring relevant observations back into review. Ask what became useful, what support remains and what needs to change.
6. **[Reassess and repeat](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/reassess-review-repeat)** — Compare abilities and context over time. Continue, adapt or complete support according to the child’s needs.
7. **[Grow independence and participation](https://www.pinnacleblooms.org/pinnacleai)** — Keep self-sufficiency, learning, relationships and mainstream participation in view. Progress is individual.
