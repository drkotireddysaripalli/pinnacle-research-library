Family discussion: An explained next step and a record of what will be checked next, with your family’s observations kept in context.

Direct answer: The Personal Development Kernel (PDK) is PinnacleAI’s child-specific record concept. It organises relevant assessed information, family priorities, goals, settings and responses over time to support authorised professional review. People interpret the record and decide the next support with the family.
Life purpose: Your child’s self-sufficient, mainstream-included life gives the work its direction. Within PinnacleAI®, PDK keeps the context behind each next step connected.
Boundary: PDK is an internal architecture concept, not a separately approved medical device or an autonomous decision-maker. This public example contains no child data. Verify publishes evidence about the system, not individual child records. Described consent and access roles do not independently establish operational security.

Illustrative example: The same child. The same shoes. Two different settings.
Fictional example: a four-year-old wants to join the school day. The family’s priority is taking part in getting ready, with comfortable and useful support. These entries explain the record concept; they are not a patient report or a measured result.
1. Compare the observations: Home · parent observation: Familiar low seat, blue hook-and-loop shoes and a three-picture sequence. The child places one foot in the shoe and closes one strap after a pointing cue. An adult steadies the shoe and helps with the other foot. A specific supported step, with the help described. It is not a claim of independent dressing. School · appropriately shared teacher observation: Busy arrival doorway, standing, with spoken instructions and no picture sequence. The child waits while the adult puts the shoes on. The record does not yet show what the child would do with familiar support here. The conditions differ. These two observations do not establish the cause or show a loss of ability.
2. Choose a specific adaptation: With the family and teacher’s agreement, the professional proposes a quieter seated arrival place, the familiar picture sequence and one consistent pointing cue. Keep help available and give the child a way to ask for help or pause.
3. Keep a checkpoint: At the agreed next review, compare the step the child initiates, the help provided, comfort and participation in both settings. Keep the observation date, contributor, proposed change and reason together. The school response to this adaptation is still pending.
4. If the adapted setting helps: Record the actual step and assistance. The professional can discuss whether to keep the arrangement or explore one next step; do not label the whole routine independent.
5. If it remains difficult or uncomfortable: Record what happened, pause if needed, and review the activity, support, timing or goal with the family. More repetition is not automatically the right response.

Questions:
What is the Personal Development Kernel?
PDK is PinnacleAI’s child-specific record concept for keeping relevant assessed information, family priorities, goals, settings and responses connected over time for authorised professional review.
What information is useful in a PDK discussion?
Bring the child’s priority, what you observed, the setting, the help provided and what happened. Relevant assessment information and appropriately shared school observations can add context; more personal detail is not automatically more useful.
What if a routine works at home but feels different at school?
Compare the conditions and support before interpreting the difference. In our illustrative shoes example, a professional proposes familiar pictures and a quieter seated place at school, then plans to review the actual response. The example does not establish the cause or a successful result.
Does PDK choose therapy without a professional?
No. PDK is a record and planning aid. A professional interprets relevant information with the family and explains the proposed support and next review. The child’s comfort, preferences and response matter.
Does a school automatically see my child’s record?
No automatic school access is implied. The source documents describe consent and authorised roles. Ask your care team what may be shared, for what purpose and who can see it in your child’s actual care arrangement.
Are children’s records public on Verify?
Verify publishes evidence about Pinnacle’s methods, licences and scope, not individual child records. The example on this page is fictional and contains no child’s personal data.
Is PDK a separately approved medical device?
PDK describes an internal record architecture within PinnacleAI. The MD-5 licence covers the named PinnacleAI GPT-OS v1.0.0 non-diagnostic developmental-support device for children aged 0–12; it is not a separate approval of PDK or proof of an individual outcome.
