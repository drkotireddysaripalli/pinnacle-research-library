Direct answer: The PDK is described in Pinnacle’s methods as an organising layer for child-specific measurements, goals, developmental history, environments and responses to activities. Life purpose: Continuity matters when a child’s team needs to see what was tried, where it worked, where it did not and what the family wants to change.
Boundary: The PDK name describes an internal architecture layer in the source methods. It is not a claim of a stand-alone regulatory approval, a public child dossier or automatic clinical judgement. Child records remain private.
