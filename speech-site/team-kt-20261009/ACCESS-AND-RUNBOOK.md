## Check and release once at meaningful boundaries

1. Match the change to a reproduced defect or approved page brief. Save a small baseline and expected behavior.
2. Build and inspect the affected source/candidate. Run focused tests that distinguish the defect; do not add implementation-mirroring tests for prose changes.
3. Before pushing, understand `.github/workflows/portal-quality.yml`: qualifying branch pushes can trigger CI/provider work. Combine the completed batch into one meaningful push. Documentation-only KT uses `[skip ci]`; this does not exempt functional releases from existing CI/BVT.
4. Integration owner refreshes live version, complete route/asset union and bindings once, integrates serially and records rollback. Do not deploy a partial portal bundle, substitute a dated asset directory or overwrite concurrent work.
5. Verify affected public URLs and the protected contracts at release; record source, deployment, exact URLs and what ran/skipped. Repeat only after a relevant change, failure or new boundary.
