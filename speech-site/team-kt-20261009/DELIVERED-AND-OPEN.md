## What actually improved

The API repair has a recorded post-change 40-minute provider window (9 October, 04:50–05:30 IST). For `www`, HTTP errors fell from **69/18,776 requests** in the comparison window to **5/11,835**; `d` from **221/713** to **0/1,220**; `books` from **28/433** to **0/622**. Traffic volumes differ, so these are observations, not a controlled attribution study. `psapi` still had **12/6,022**, and `tvapi` **87/160** errors in the post window. The whole origin estate is not fixed. Source: `deployment/origin-repair-followthrough-20261009.json` (existing restricted/local evidence where not in Git).

The acquisition repairs demonstrate reliable mechanics on their tested paths: accepted receipts, source retention and correct interaction events. They do not yet prove incremental qualified calls or admissions. The 9 October 00:00–05:44 IST inspected receipt cohort had zero accepted records; this is not a total-network lead count. Do not reuse it as a later live metric.
