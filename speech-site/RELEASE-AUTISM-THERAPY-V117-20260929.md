## Evidence and claim boundaries

- PinnacleAI GPT-OS v1.0.0 remains non-diagnostic developmental-support Class B SaMD for children aged 0–12. It does not diagnose autism or prove an individual therapy result.
- Regulatory and quality records establish their documented scope. No cure, normalisation, guaranteed speech, school admission, mainstream placement, independence, universal service availability, fixed timeframe or individual outcome is claimed.
- The 160Yrs Paradigm Shift is Pinnacle's life-first thesis. External WHO, NICE, CDC and RCI sources provide context and do not endorse Pinnacle.
- Every autistic child does not need every therapy. Assessment, child and family priorities and relevant professional judgement determine the route.
