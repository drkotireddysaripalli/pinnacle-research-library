### Design and source use

- Six short cards, each with an accessible book/ear/play/pathway icon, descriptive title, institution/country and one useful sentence. Three columns on desktop, two on tablet and one on narrow phones; comfortable touch targets and visible focus. Use Pinnacle's accepted colours and type. No copied government logos, seals or endorsement badges.
- Place the curated block with practical resources before the final FAQs/close. Also use selected inline government citations beside the actual general-guidance claim. The block stays secondary to the Pinnacle explanation and clear call/assessment action.
- Replace existing ASHA general-guidance anchors in Speech with a relevant NIDCD/CDC/AIISH source where it supports the sentence. The ASHA Communication Bill of Rights is not interchangeable with a milestone checklist: remove that redundant outgoing label or rewrite/source its surrounding specific claim accurately. Preserve the child's communication choices in the narrative.
- External parent-guidance links lead directly to government-owned resources. ASHA, private providers/charities, commercial directories, Wikipedia and intergovernmental organisations are excluded from this block under the owner's preference. Government pages may themselves contain external references; do not import those references as Pinnacle's curated links.
- Use an ordinary crawlable `<a href>` with a meaningful label; no forced pop-up, tracking redirect, heavy embed or automatic `nofollow` solely because the source is external. Mark a new-window link accessibly if one is deliberately used.
- Write original short summaries and source specific statements rather than copying checklists or full articles. Keep actual source age ranges visible; a birth-to-five resource is not evidence for every age shown in our creative.
- These resources support general guidance, not Pinnacle outcomes, licensing, government affiliation or foreign healthcare entitlement. One concise “Independent public guidance” label is sufficient; do not blanket the page with defensive disclaimers.

Outgoing government citations provide useful reference paths. They are not backlinks earned by Pinnacle and do not establish endorsement or an automatic ranking boost. The complementary off-page task is to earn relevant citations to our substantive original evidence and helpful service/visit resources through legitimate destinations, recorded separately after publication.
