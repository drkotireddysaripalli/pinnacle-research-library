## Locked seven stages from existing assessment source

Local source: `src/data/assessment-content.ts` (current source inspected).

1. **Understand abilities** — strengths, everyday observations and suitable assessment; read AbilityScore with age, context and skills. `/abilityscore`
2. **Plan for readiness** — agree useful priorities for what the child/family wants to make possible; readiness views support judgement. `/seven-readiness-indexes`
3. **Choose relevant support** — therapies, educational support or health review that fit this child; every child does not need every service. `/autism-therapy`
4. **Practise in everyday life** — manageable guided practice in meaningful routines. `/everyday-therapy`
5. **Track and correct** — bring observations back; ask what became useful, what support remains and what should change. `/fusion-module`
6. **Reassess and repeat** — compare abilities and context; continue, adapt or complete support according to need. `/reassess-review-repeat`
7. **Grow independence and participation** — keep self-sufficiency, learning, relationships and mainstream participation in view; individual progress. `/pinnacleai`

## Claims and product details to leave out of the refreshed parent proposition

- Uncorroborated WHO, Stanford, Heidelberg, Singapore IMH collaboration/study/adoption; UN draft appearances; global standard/reference-architecture recognition; unsourced awards or media quotes.
- “World’s first/most complete/unmatched”, universal metric/success prediction, granted/global patents or patents in 160+ countries. The current Verify research context distinguishes application-stage IP.
- Diagnosis by AbilityScore/PinnacleAI; interpreting Study/IQ readiness as an established IQ test; independent completed external validation. The public AbilityScore methodology is preliminary, and v2.1 external-validation protocol reports planned work.
- Guaranteed cure, graduation, independence, school admission, forecast timing, or a universal score threshold deciding care. Support and accommodations can remain relevant as abilities grow.
- Identifiable child stories or attributed parent quotations whose consent and provenance were not supplied. Current language about silent eyes, helplessness, restraint and parents waiting too late also conflicts with the dignity/capability direction.
- Mixed old scale units: 19M/21M sessions, 70 cities/centres, ten studies with twelve listed. Prefer no scale headline on this explanation. If needed later, use dated defined services and registration measures from the separate assurance sources, never an outcome count.
- Universal free screening/assessment. Only a campaign-specific confirmed offer may be stated; the assessment source distinguishes the speech/language campaign.
- Exact automatic goal-rating/replacement thresholds, practice quantities, advance-delivery lead time, language/video availability, primary-caregiver permission behaviour, fixed learning thresholds or predictive accuracy. Live Verify preserves these as described mechanisms with explicit version/UI evidence gates; a source-linked general mechanism can be explained without claiming the interface was demonstrated.
- The 97% statement is not needed for this page’s proposition. Current Verify keeps it as an owner-requested claim pending endpoint/population/method support; an activity audit does not establish it.
