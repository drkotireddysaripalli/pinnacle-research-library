It uses the existing shared branded shell rather than a separate header/footer.

## What changed in the narrative

The page says **“Understand what is happening. Help your child take part.”** before it discusses technique or software. It answers “What is ABA?” directly, then uses a transition-and-break example to show observation, a useful agreed goal, support in real life and review. The seven-stage path makes the child’s growing self-sufficiency and mainstream participation the direction for suitable methods and people. The first-call block says exactly what the team can check. Three distinct on-page scenes and a readable 1200 × 630 social creative reinforce those decisions; source records and licence limits stay in HTML rather than inside image-only text.

On a phone, the common header still occupies about 226 px at 320 px, so the supporting hero paragraph begins below the first viewport. The headline, brand scene and sticky call remain visible. The More menu now starts with therapies, then start actions, then the longer proof list. The seven-stage layout was widened from an initially cramped seven-column desktop row to readable 4+2+1 cards; it becomes two columns on tablet and one on phone.

## Search and evidence boundary

The live page has one H1, a service-specific title/description, self-canonical, direct 301 aliases, an updated child sitemap entry, OG/Twitter image, eleven visible/schema-matched FAQs, and JSON/text/Markdown source exports. The `Service` schema does not assert blanket India-wide ABA availability. Source links and related-service links are contextual. Google/Bing crawl eligibility, IndexNow receipt and machine-readable output do **not** establish indexing of this revision, search position, AI citation, leads or visits.

The established slug contains “best” and “proven-improvement-rate”, but the page does not claim a ranking or unverified ABA improvement rate. A URL migration should be assessed only after current Search Console and backlink baseline review. The public MD-5/BIS records describe PinnacleAI® non-diagnostic software and cannot be read as an ABA professional licence or outcome endorsement. The 62 locations are a network directory, not proof that each offers ABA.
