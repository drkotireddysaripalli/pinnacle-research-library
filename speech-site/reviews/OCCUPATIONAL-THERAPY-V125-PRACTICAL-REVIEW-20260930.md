## What the deployed page actually delivers

- The opening says “Occupational therapy for children. More ways to take part in everyday life.” It places a working `tel:+919100181181` action and the free **telephone-guidance** cue in the first phone screen. It does not say the assessment, therapy or appointment is free.
- A parent can move from a daily concern through professional assessment, a suitable OT goal, family-guided practice and review. The child’s self-sufficiency and mainstream participation remain a direction of care, not a promised graduation or software score.
- The family scenes, mealtime example, seven connected HTML stages and mother/professional review image give the life-first narrative visual shape. Eight visible FAQs match the eight schema questions. The approved 1200×630 branded OG image and source-map JSON/text/Markdown remain public.
- Source placement is disciplined: **31,052,382** means dated network service volume, not an OT outcome; MD-5/BIS describe non-diagnostic software scope, not therapist credentials or a guaranteed result. The general centre directory contains **62 listings**, not a verified 62-location OT roster.
- The common compact header retains Verify, Citations, full More navigation and the national number. The Verify gateway is in the common footer. Ten managed routes rendered matching header/footer markup after the v125 build; this is a shared-source result, not a separate per-page patch.

## Comparison with Speech Therapy

Speech has the more immediately emotional opening and a separately confirmed FREE speech-assessment offer. OT more clearly explains what its focal service can contribute and what a first call can and cannot arrange. OT should retain that honest difference; it should **not** import Speech's offer or claim Speech's outcome evidence as OT-specific proof. Both use the same header and footer sources. Another batch of badges, schema citations or poster variants would add little without a specific comprehension or share defect.

## Highest-value remaining work

1. **Operations:** maintain a dated location-level OT service, professional role, appointment and fee register. Until verified, keep “call to confirm” wording and do not mark all 62 listings as OT centres.
2. **Care handoff:** have the receiving telephone team read back the page's OT promise and demonstrate the path to a suitable visit. Capture answered call, appointment and attendance independently of dial clicks; check the real enrolment/CRM receipt separately.
3. **Human/device evidence:** run short, uncoached family tasks and a real iPhone/Safari, screen-reader/reduced-motion and field Core Web Vitals pass. Fix concrete confusion or access defects discovered there.
4. **Search observation:** inspect the exact URL in Search Console/Bing after enough time for pickup; distinguish crawl, index, query/rank, referral and AI citation. Consider a cleaner canonical only with a backlink and Search Console migration case because the existing slug contains `best` and `proven-improvement-rate` while the page correctly avoids an OT-specific improvement-rate claim.
