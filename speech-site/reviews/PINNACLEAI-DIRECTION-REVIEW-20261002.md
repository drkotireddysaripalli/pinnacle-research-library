2 October 2026. Read-only review of the live V163 page. This records observed issues and proposed work; it is not a deployment receipt or a new replacement for the governing page work order.

The /pinnacleai page is narrated from PinnacleAI's central standpoint. The separate Paradigm Shift page owns the fuller philosophy. The child and family remain the beneficiaries and purpose; PinnacleAI is the subject explaining how measurement, planning, therapies, home practice, tracking and reassessment work toward the child's self-sufficient, mainstream-included life. They contain useful principles but are not an executable Astro page-direction pipeline. Their scope and historical facts must not override the current owner instructions, governing work order or live evidence sources.

## Recommended production direction

Keep one accountable owner and one consolidated independent review. Use the existing work order as the requirement source. A proposed page-direction skill should make that work order executable; it must not introduce a competing standard or another review committee.

For every block, bind these decisions together before implementation:

1. The reader's question and what they should understand or feel afterwards.
2. The page subject's role: for this page, what PinnacleAI actually contributes.
3. Exact heading, short explanation and optional detail.
4. Specific source-backed proof with the matching Verify link.
5. Visual form: poster, labelled diagram, example, icon row, comparison or typography.
6. Approved asset reuse or a complete branded native ChatGPT image prompt, dimensions and phone treatment.
7. HTML meaning, source order, accessibility and machine-readable relationships.
8. Next action, relevant page link and proximity of the phone/enrolment path.

Figma MCP is the first optional external addition to pilot for the visual board and fidelity. Keep native session image generation, the existing Astro components, source-controlled common shell, existing build/deploy pipeline and TestingBot. A browser-rendered local storyboard remains sufficient to proceed if Figma is not connected. Measure the pilot by accepted first-pass sections, fidelity and rework; do not claim time, credit, search or conversion improvements without observation.

No new skill or tool integration was created in this review. The PinnacleAI narrative correction and the bounded tablet header defect are ready to incorporate into the next authorised implementation revision.
