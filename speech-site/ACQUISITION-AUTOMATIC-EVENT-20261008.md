# Acquisition: automatic accepted events and the protected business record

## What must happen

A valid durable accepted receipt produces one first-party business record automatically. The browser sends `enquiry_accepted` immediately on that receipt. An undecided visitor uses denied analytics/advertising storage; explicit refusal and Global Privacy Control suppress Google transport. Optional browsing analytics and its source storage still require permission. Vendor failure cannot prevent calling, intake or the honest receipt confirmation.

An accepted enquiry is not a connected call, qualification, appointment, attendance or admission. Those require authoritative dated operational records.

## Implementation

- Shared receipt listener uses a fixed, coarse event vocabulary. No receipt/request IDs, name, mobile, message, child details or private topic enters Google. The undecided payload also excludes campaign/click IDs and referrer.
- SDK starts only when optional analytics is permitted or an eligible real receipt arrives. Default storage is denied; advertising signals/personalisation remain off. A subsequent optional choice cannot replay the same receipt.
- The receiver's existing unique-key transaction remains the automatic business authority. A bounded **private RPC** projects that ledger into counts and exact protected intake references, with permitted campaign evidence, timestamps, explicit truncation and unknown downstream states. No database migration, extra customer write, public reporting endpoint or new access binding is introduced.
- The short callback and practical visit/fee explanation extend to OT, ABA and Autism alongside 59 domestic centre pages, the directory and Speech information: **64 inline callback pages**, plus the revised enrolment client.

Other key events include page/contact activity; these are not proof of received clinic enquiries. A newer release or local wire interception does not create historical Google records.
