## Reader journey and required blocks

1. **Recognise Pinnacle here:** centre name/emblem, locality, autism/child-development support, life benefit, primary national call and assessment action. Exact address and secondary local contact easy to reach on mobile. Exterior/nameboard is the preferred opening image.
2. **Find the place:** address, postcode, landmark, Google Business listing, directions, click-to-load map, entrance/access/appointment preparation. Hours only when actually supplied, never helpline hours used as clinic hours.
3. **Life as the direction:** self-sufficiency and mainstream participation. PinnacleAI measurement, plan, relevant therapies, home practice, tracking/correction and review serve that direction. Family is a participant throughout.
4. **Network credibility at the local decision:** source-linked service/registration figures, software licences, original evidence and UDIN routes in Verify. Preserve defined scope and dates; do not turn network service volume into branch outcomes.
5. **Understand core therapies:** speech, occupational, ABA/behavioural, special education and integrated autism. Clear useful contributions and links to complete therapy pages; individual support follows assessment.
6. **One concrete everyday example:** preserve curated branch examples; otherwise select a complete strengths-based communication, routine, play or learning example. Explain observation, goal, action, home transfer and review without invented patient outcomes.
7. **First assessment/visit:** what to share, reports/observations to bring, professional, service, fees, appointment, access and plan. Keep the existing receiving system and real centre routing value.
8. **Seven connected stages:** readable cards connecting directly to PinnacleAI system pages, therapies, self-sufficiency and mainstream life. No horizontally crushed strips.
9. **The real centre:** exterior, interior and local emblem, proper captions, responsive images and source identity. Never substitute another branch's room. Extend missing media only from matched supplied source.
10. **Centre walkthrough:** exact matched YouTube video or supplied centre video, user-initiated embed, external viewing fallback, no autoplay or heavy initial video download.
11. **The people and their promise:** official network staff-promise playlist. Identify network content separately from named branch staff; no invented local roster.
12. **Families speaking for themselves:** official parent/AbilityScore/PinnacleAI and child-experience playlists. Label individual experiences, not universal results or fabricated branch testimonials. No new public child identities are created.
13. **Google reviews:** branch listing/read-review action, separate leave-review action, honest supplied rating/count snapshot where usable. Current reviews may be embedded/quoted only from an actual retrieved source. No self-serving LocalBusiness review-star markup or fake review widget.
14. **Catchment:** real nearby localities and supplied postcodes in useful arrival/choice context. No repetitive keyword piles, invented catchments or arbitrary doorway pages.
15. **Nearby centres:** validated approximate straight-line distances, actual branch links, clear city/address and separate status treatment. No guessed road times; preserve held campaign locations.
16. **All centres:** the existing searchable network directory and links between centre detail pages; one source register drives both.
17. **Continue learning:** Ask, Sunshine, FAQ, Materials and intervention/family resources. Link live useful destinations; keep unavailable techniques endpoint accountable until its source restoration, rather than claim it works.
18. **Shop and books:** relevant parent learning resources, national Shop/Books links, format/offer detail on their existing pages. Do not imply a compulsory purchase or alter catalogue, stock or payment records.
19. **Verify and citations:** readable local fact trail, source dates, evidence JSON/text/Markdown and vCard. Stable canonical, coherent Organization/Brand/branch graph, actual address/geo/phone, visible FAQ parity, OG/Twitter and share links.
20. **Close the loop:** call and centre-selected assessment enquiry, WhatsApp sharing, copy/save contact. Consent-based shared measurement; navigation, call clicks, accepted enquiries, qualified calls, walk-ins and admissions remain distinct.
