## 1. The job of the page

### Owner correction: preserve the movement and its language

The governing proposition is **PinnacleAI® Paradigm Shift: the child's self-sufficient, mainstream-included life determines the entire system from the start.** It determines the abilities we assess, goals we set, methods we choose, people we bring together, everyday practice, tracking, correction and reassessment. Self-Sufficient and Mainstream are substantial, named pillars. Everyday examples make the ambition tangible; they must not replace it as the headline proposition. The circular creative must show both the purpose and the whole mechanism, with the child and family at the centre.

Keep the movement's home-and-parent ambition explicit: **Our mission: transform every home in need into a place where development continues, and equip every parent to guide their child's journey toward a self-sufficient, mainstream-included life.** Explain the professional demonstration, everyday practice and feedback that make the mission practical. Do not imply a parent gains a professional licence.

Evidence should be prominent and specific: original licences, named software functions, defined operational scale, research and independently reported figures, linked to Verify. Preserve strong supported claims; apply source qualifications to the particular claim, rather than weakening the whole narrative. Do not invent exclusivity, historical claims or guaranteed individual outcomes.

Speak as Pinnacle to the mother, father, family, caregiver, teacher and professional. Show why a child's self-sufficient, mainstream-included life determines the abilities, goals, methods, people, practice and review from the beginning. Bring the child and family into the centre of a visibly connected circle. Sell an understandable, credible way to begin with Pinnacle: call 9100 181 181, discuss the suitable professional and centre, arrange a visit and consider enrolment.

Recognition → life purpose → understandable mechanism → relevant evidence → family agency → practical next step. The primary reader should not need to learn product vocabulary before seeing its value.

Review lenses: parent/family, child participation, multidisciplinary professionals, sales/business development, brand/design, regulatory/source integrity, accessibility/performance, search/retrieval and conversion measurement.
