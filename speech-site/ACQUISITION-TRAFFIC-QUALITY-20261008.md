## Classification that is defensible

1. **Relevant and progressing:** permitted engagement/contact followed by an actual receipt or operational outcome.
2. **Relevant, no recorded contact:** a useful visit without known conversion. This can be a parent researching or returning later.
3. **Landing friction:** errors, confusing destination, slow render or unusable contact control supported by reproducible evidence.
4. **Irrelevant acquisition:** search term, location or placement demonstrably outside the approved service/audience need. Review exclusions against genuine parent language and confirmed services.
5. **Suspicious activity requiring review:** documented automation, abnormal bursts, repeated abuse or invalid submissions supported by independent security/vendor evidence. A low duration, repeat visit, shared IP or absent lead alone is insufficient.
6. **Google-confirmed invalid activity / posted credit:** use the exact Google report, billing period and adjustment. Never equate vendor flags with charged clicks or refunds.

Phishing concerns require actual impersonation, credential theft or malicious content evidence. Browsing behavior does not establish phishing. Repeated visits without contact do not establish intentional sabotage.
