## Page outcome

Help a parent or caregiver see how occupational therapy may support the child's participation in everyday life and understand why Pinnacle starts with the life the child is growing toward. The page must connect therapy-specific work to greater independence in relevant routines, learning, play, self-care and participation without guaranteeing a particular outcome.

The page must feel as complete, persuasive and evidence-connected as the released Speech Therapy page while remaining authentically occupational-therapy specific.

## Visitor questions to answer

1. Could occupational therapy be relevant to the everyday difficulty I am seeing?
2. What does occupational therapy support in a child's life?
3. What happens during the first conversation or assessment?
4. How does Pinnacle decide what to work on and why?
5. How are therapy, home, school and family observations connected?
6. How is progress reviewed and the plan adjusted?
7. What evidence supports the PinnacleAI®, AbilityScore® and readiness explanations?
8. Which centre can help and how do I begin?

## Required narrative

Adapt the common portal narrative to occupational therapy:

1. Begin with everyday participation and growing independence, not diagnostic labels or a list of exercises.
2. Recognise practical parent concerns using respectful, capability-focused language.
3. Explain occupational therapy's role in plain language after confirming the current service scope.
4. Show that an individual activity gains meaning from the child's whole-life priority.
5. Explain the Pinnacle life-first difference: desired life → capabilities → goals → people/methods → therapy/everyday practice → observation → review.
6. Follow one clearly labelled illustrative occupational goal into therapy, home or school and back into review.
7. Present the seven-stage lifecycle without turning it into a guaranteed clinical pathway.
8. Show family participation as informed agency, not homework, blame or a substitute for professional care.
9. Bring verified centres, first-visit clarity and the practical next step into view before the deepest evidence section.
10. Use Verify citations beside relevant claims, then provide a complete source path for visitors who want to inspect more.
