Three questions.
A clearer starting point.

Credentials are most useful when they help you ask informed questions about the support your child will receive.

01

Is this the right document?

Match the name of the product or centre, its address and the activity covered. Ask for the latest copy for the place you will visit.

What can it tell me?

A licence describes permitted activity. A quality certificate describes a system. A study explores a specific question in a defined group of children.

HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

Evidence with
its meaning intact.

A record is useful when you can see both what it supports and the limits of that support.

01 / PROVENANCE

Start with the source

Original documents take priority over cover letters, brochures and website summaries. The review date describes our inspection, not a fresh confirmation from the issuer.

02 / SCOPE

Match the exact entity

Read the named company, product, address and permitted activity. A product licence does not establish the registration of every therapy centre.

03 / MEANING

Keep each claim precise

Business identity, quality systems, clinical evidence and government appreciation serve different purposes. Ask what each one means for your child’s plan; individual outcomes vary.

Evidence status, explained

Original reviewed
A supplied original or primary publication record was inspected. Current status and independent validation are separate checks.
Registry record matched
The exact entity and identifier were checked against an official registry on the review date.
Company-published
Published by Pinnacle or its authors. This label does not imply independent validation.
Needs follow-up
A source disagreement, missing document or claim needs another check. The record explains the next step.
Source needed
No suitable original was available for this review. This does not mean a credential does not exist.
Review boundaries & publication notes +

This is a private review edition prepared on 15 September 2026. The page does not represent a regulator’s certification service. Live registry matches are counted only when an exact record has been checked against an official registry source.

Public document copies preserve issuer, entity, reference, scope and dates while removing personal identifiers and signatures where appropriate. A document’s stated validity is recorded separately from a live check for suspension, cancellation or changes. View file-integrity records.

Research protocols describe planned methods. Company summaries and operational totals are not substitutes for completed study results with methods, populations, outcomes and limitations.
