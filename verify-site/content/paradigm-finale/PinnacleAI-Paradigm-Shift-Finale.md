## See what the session is for. Before it begins.

Selected anonymised excerpts from a supplied PinnacleAI® goal set and session. These are planned goals and activities, not achieved outcomes.

### Ask for what I need

**Life purpose:** Ask for help. Make a choice. Say when to stop. These are abilities a child can use throughout the day.

**Proposed goal:** Over the next 3–4 weeks, use at least five functional spoken words or consistent approximations, supported by natural pointing or gestures when needed, across eight of ten communication opportunities.

**Session practice:** Two seven-minute activities practise choosing, requesting, help, rejection and continuation.

**Family practice:** Model one useful word during play or a daily routine. Accept approximations and gestures; honour “no” or “stop”.

**Record for review:** Record what the child communicated, the form used, the support needed and whether the response was spontaneous.

#### Plan-defined achievement criteria

These labels are not observed results or response-accuracy percentages.

- **10% plan level:** A vocalisation, word attempt or point in 1 of 10 opportunities after a full model.
- **25% plan level:** Two functional words or approximations in 3 of 10 opportunities with repeated models.
- **45% plan level:** Three target words or approximations in 5 of 10 opportunities with a verbal prompt.
- **70% plan level:** Four functional words across at least three purposes in 7 of 10 opportunities, with no more than one model.
- **90% plan level:** All five target words or approximations in 8 of 10 opportunities with minimal prompting in therapy and structured parent practice.
- **100% plan level:** At least five functional words or stable approximations used independently at 80% success across three consecutive sessions and two daily home routines.

### Share a moment

**Life purpose:** Notice a communication partner, enjoy a shared activity and take a turn—without making eye contact a requirement.

**Proposed goal:** Over the next 3–4 weeks, orient toward a communication partner, respond to name and remain jointly engaged with a person and preferred activity for at least ten seconds in eight of ten opportunities, without forced eye contact.

**Session practice:** A six-minute child-led play activity and a seven-minute name-response and turn-taking routine.

**Family practice:** Call the child’s name once before a pleasant activity. Allow time to respond and recognise orientation toward the person or activity.

**Record for review:** Distinguish independent responses from verbal, gestural and missed responses. Record the duration of shared engagement.

#### Plan-defined achievement criteria

These labels are not observed results or response-accuracy percentages.

- **10% plan level:** Brief orientation for two seconds in 1 of 10 opportunities with full verbal and gestural support.
- **25% plan level:** Response to name or joining an adult’s activity for three seconds in 3 of 10 opportunities with repeated prompts.
- **45% plan level:** Shared engagement for five seconds in 5 of 10 opportunities, with at least two name responses during a routine.
- **70% plan level:** Shared engagement for 7–10 seconds in 7 of 10 opportunities with no more than two prompts.
- **90% plan level:** Response to name and at least ten seconds of engagement in 8 of 10 opportunities with minimal prompting during structured sessions.
- **100% plan level:** Ten seconds of shared engagement and response to name in at least 8 of 10 opportunities across three consecutive sessions and two familiar home routines.

### Understand everyday words

**Life purpose:** Understand familiar objects, actions and a simple instruction in the routines where they matter.

**Proposed goal:** Over the next 3–4 weeks, identify at least eight familiar objects and four action words, and follow five familiar one-step instructions with 80% success as verbal and gestural cues are gradually reduced.

**Session practice:** Six minutes with familiar real objects and seven minutes practising action words and one-step instructions.

**Family practice:** Give one familiar instruction, then allow a five-second pause. Keep the words and cues consistent with the session guidance.

**Record for review:** Record object or action, choice-set size, prompt level and accuracy. Review action words and instruction-following separately.

#### Plan-defined achievement criteria

These labels are not observed results or response-accuracy percentages.

- **10% plan level:** Attend to and touch one named familiar object in 1 of 10 opportunities with full modelling.
- **25% plan level:** Identify two familiar objects from a choice of two in 3 of 10 opportunities and follow one modelled instruction.
- **45% plan level:** Identify four objects and two demonstrated actions with 50% success, and follow two familiar one-step instructions.
- **70% plan level:** Identify six objects and three actions, and follow four one-step instructions in 7 of 10 opportunities with cues.
- **90% plan level:** Identify eight objects and four actions, and follow five one-step instructions in 8 of 10 opportunities with minimal cues.
- **100% plan level:** At least 80% success across three consecutive sessions and two home routines.

### The supplied 40-minute session

1. **Child-led joint engagement — 6 minutes.** Choose a preferred activity; the therapist follows the child’s interest, pauses and observes shared engagement. Eight brief opportunities. Record: Independent, verbally cued, gesturally cued and missed responses; approximately ten seconds of engagement.

2. **Familiar-object identification — 6 minutes.** Use real objects, one clear instruction and processing time. Ten mixed opportunities with object positions varied. Record: Object, number of choices, prompt level and accuracy.

3. **Choosing and requesting — 7 minutes.** Offer a choice, model a useful word and accept a purposeful approximation. Eight opportunities to request, choose or continue. Record: Communication purpose, spoken form, prompt level and whether spontaneous.

4. **Name response and turn-taking — 7 minutes.** Use an enjoyable shared activity, call the name once and allow time. Eight opportunities, naturally spaced; eye contact is not required. Record: Independent orientation within 3–5 seconds and responses requiring cues.

5. **Action words and one-step instructions — 7 minutes.** Demonstrate action meanings and use familiar instructions in play. Ten mixed trials; reduce support as appropriate. Record: Action-word identification and instruction-following separately.

6. **Help, stop, more and choice — 7 minutes.** Create safe, natural opportunities to communicate. Honour purposeful gestures, approximations and stopping requests. Eight opportunities. Record: Spontaneous words, approximations, gestures, functions and prompt levels.

A separate five-minute family handover covers what happened and home practice. No completed response or reassessment is asserted. The seven supplied goals cover shared engagement; familiar objects/actions/instructions; functional communication; vocabulary; greetings and social attention; where questions; and short word combinations. Six activities target the first three goals, twice each. This source is separate from the assessment-report example.
