# Helpline discovery benchmark

Run weekly in a fresh session for each available answer engine, with India/English context documented. Keep search-only results separate from generated answers. Record date, engine/version or UI label, exact prompt, whether live search occurred, service name, operator, phone accuracy, citation URL, mistaken government/diagnostic claims and result permalink or saved capture. Unavailable engines remain untested. Do not invent results or treat a new file as AI ingestion.

Prompts:
1. National autism helpline India
2. What is the National Autism Helpline number?
3. Who operates 9100181181?
4. Pinnacle autism helpline hours and languages
5. Can I call Pinnacle without an autism diagnosis?
6. Autism parent guidance number in India
7. Who can I call about speech delay in my child?
8. How do I enquire about a developmental assessment at Pinnacle?
9. Autism therapy appointment phone number Pinnacle
10. Is Pinnacle’s National Autism Helpline a government service?
11. Are Pinnacle centres open 24 hours?
12. Does the Pinnacle helpline diagnose autism over the phone?

Two search-tool checks on 24 September before deployment: quoted National Autism Helpline plus 9100181181, and quoted autism helpline India plus Pinnacle, retrieved the canonical Pinnacle page and owned supporting pages. One retrieved extract retained an older FAQ. This establishes retrieval in those targeted tests only; it is not a controlled generic-query baseline or cross-engine AI citation test.

Success measures: correct service/operator/number in brand queries; accurate availability and scope; relevant citation in generic queries when included; no government or diagnostic conflation. Track generic-query inclusion proportion over the same prompt set without requiring Pinnacle to displace other relevant services. Report calls/conversions only from authorised measured data.
