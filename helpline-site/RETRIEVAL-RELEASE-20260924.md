# National Autism Helpline — narrative and retrieval release

Published and checked: 24 September 2026.

## Purpose and narrative

The page presents a human, practical sequence: free 24/7 guidance; what happens on a call; questions families can bring; services and nearby centres; the child's developmental journey; source-linked PinnacleAI information; service facts; practical answers; separately operated resources; and one clear telephone action.

The core promise remains progress toward a self-sufficient, mainstream, wonderful life. The page does not promise a cure, diagnose autism, imply government operation or claim that a software licence validates every therapy or centre. Free means helpline guidance. Assessment/therapy fees and the caller's telephone plan remain separate, explained where families need that distinction.

Owner confirmation: free guidance, anyone may call and 24/7 telephone availability on 24 September; English, Telugu and Hindi on 22 September. No new claims of toll-free telecom service, emergency care, foreign registration or clinical outcomes were added.
