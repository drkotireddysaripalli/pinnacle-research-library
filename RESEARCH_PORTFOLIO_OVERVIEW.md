## Portfolio-wide boundaries

- The portfolio contains six preprints and one report. It does not represent seven peer-reviewed journal articles.
- PINNACLE-VAL is a retrospective OSF registration and a companion to the psychometric protocol, not an eighth study.
- Planned sample sizes, cohorts and analysis targets are not completed samples or results.
- The AbilityScore technical monograph presents preliminary operational evidence; it does not establish a completed concurrent-validity result.
- The RCI repository versions describe one study and dataset release, despite multiple public file formats and versions.
- Professional affiliation is Bharath Healthcare Laboratories Private Limited. Indian School of Business is completed online executive education, not a degree or employment appointment.
