# Research metadata and reproducibility index

This index collects machine-readable aids for citing, attributing, deduplicating and interpreting the seven-work portfolio. The canonical Zenodo records remain the source of record.

## Citation and identity

- [Seven canonical citations in CSL-JSON](CANONICAL_CITATIONS_CSL.json) — exact titles, author order, dates, DOI, URL, type and version provenance.
- [Author and contributor role map](AUTHOR_CONTRIBUTOR_ROLE_MAP.csv) — preserves creator order and keeps Sreeja Reddy Saripalli's PGAF editor role distinct from authorship.
- [Canonical work relationship map](CANONICAL_WORK_RELATIONSHIP_MAP.csv) — records explicit citation, version and retrospective-registration edges without inflating the work count.
- [Evidence-status vocabulary](EVIDENCE_STATUS_VOCABULARY.csv) — defines protocol, registration, preprint, report, public, indexed and independently cited states.

## RCI register analysis

- [Reproduction checklist](RCI_REPRODUCTION_CHECKLIST.md) — source checksum, environment, validation and interpretation checks.
- [Counting rules](RCI_COUNTING_RULES.csv) — analytical-unit, serial, geography, label and version-scope rules.
- [Limitations matrix](RCI_LIMITATIONS_MATRIX.csv) — supported descriptions and the inferences the source cannot support.
- [Full reproduction guide](RCI_DATA_README.md) and [portable script](reproduce_workforce.py).

## Evidence boundaries

Six canonical works are preprints and PGAF is a report. Protocols do not imply results. PINNACLE-VAL's OSF record is retrospective because some planned analyses had already run. The monograph provides preliminary operational evidence rather than completed formal validation. RCI-listed institution codes do not establish active training capacity or clinician supply. Public availability does not establish search indexing, independent citation or endorsement.
