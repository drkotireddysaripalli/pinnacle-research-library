# Portfolio deduplication rules

These rules keep repository manifestations, registrations and supporting resources from inflating the seven-work portfolio.

1. Count a work by its underlying research question and study/report identity, not by the number of platforms that display it.
2. Treat the RCI Research Square v1 DOI (`10.21203/rs.3.rs-11043330/v1`) and Zenodo v2.0 DOI (`10.5281/zenodo.22761810`) as two manifestations of one underlying study. Cite the version actually used.
3. Treat OSF registration `10.17605/OSF.IO/78WF5` as a retrospective registration companion to PINNACLE-VAL, not as an additional research output or prospectively registered study.
4. Treat GitHub files, profile listings, reader stories, discussions and citation exports as dissemination or reproducibility resources. They are not additional studies and do not create independent citations.
5. Keep a protocol distinct from its future results publication. A protocol record does not imply that recruitment, analysis or reporting has been completed.
6. Keep PGAF contributor roles distinct: Koti Reddy Saripalli is the sole deposited creator, Sreeja Reddy Saripalli is an editor, and Koti Group SEVA Foundation is a research-group contributor.
7. Do not merge records solely because titles are similar, and do not claim a namesake record without exact identity evidence.
8. Record later versions as version relationships. Do not count a revised deposit twice unless it reports a materially distinct study with its own methods and results.

The machine-readable relationship evidence is in `CANONICAL_WORK_RELATIONSHIP_MAP.csv`; deposited creator/contributor evidence is in `AUTHOR_CONTRIBUTOR_ROLE_MAP.csv`.
