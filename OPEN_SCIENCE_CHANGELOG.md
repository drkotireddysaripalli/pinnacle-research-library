## Evidence boundaries carried across every release

Six canonical works are preprints and PGAF is a report. PINNACLE-VAL and PINNACLE-TRAJ are protocols rather than completed results. PINNACLE-VAL's OSF registration is retrospective, and n=300/60 are planned targets rather than achieved samples. The AbilityScore monograph is preliminary operational evidence; no completed formal concurrent-validity result is published. The participation review is unregistered and has no results. RCI repository versions remain one underlying study, and register counts do not establish approval, active capacity, workforce supply, quality or population-adjusted access. PGAF claims remain source-attributed.

Commit history is the authoritative record of exact file changes and dates.
