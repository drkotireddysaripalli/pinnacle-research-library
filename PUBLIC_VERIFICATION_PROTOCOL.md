# Public research-record verification protocol

Use this protocol after every scholarly-profile, repository or dissemination change.

1. Capture the signed-in saved state: destination, exact field or record, URL, time and visible success signal.
2. Open the exact route in a logged-out or otherwise independent session. Record whether the content renders, redirects to login, is pending moderation or is unavailable.
3. Match a record-specific marker such as the exact title, DOI, author, version, resource label or correction text. A generic profile shell is insufficient.
4. Keep the states separate: `prepared`, `submitted`, `approved`, `saved`, `public`, `search-indexed` and `independently cited` are not interchangeable.
5. Verify version relationships before counting works. In particular, Research Square RCI v1 and Zenodo RCI v2.0 are one study with two manifestations.
6. Verify identity with ORCID and deposited metadata. Do not claim a namesake work from initials or surname similarity.
7. Preserve output type and limits: preprint, protocol, report, retrospective registration, preliminary evidence and no-results states.
8. Do not infer search indexing from public availability. Record the search engine and exact result only when indexing is directly observed.
9. Do not infer independent citation from profile inclusion, repository links, social posts or platform metrics. Inspect the citing scholarly work and context.
10. Do not resubmit a pending correction or repeat a blocked access attempt without a material state change.

For each completed run, retain a numbered ledger with destination, action, URL or receipt, source and verification state.
